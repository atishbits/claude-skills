# Categories

Each transaction gets one category. Flow categories are not spending; the report keeps them
apart so transfers and investments are never counted as money spent.

**Flows (not spending)**

| Category | Meaning |
|---|---|
| income | Salary and other earnings |
| interest | Interest and dividends paid to you |
| refund | Money returned for a purchase; reduces spending |
| own_transfer | Between your own accounts |
| card_payment | Paying a card bill from a bank account, or that payment arriving on the card |
| family_transfer | Money sent to family; an outflow, shown apart from your spending |
| tax | Income tax paid |
| investment:fd, :stocks, :mf, :bonds, :gold, :other | Money into (or back from) investments |

**Spending**

fuel, groceries, food_delivery, dining, shopping, utilities, housing, travel, transport, health,
education, insurance, subscriptions, entertainment, fees_charges, cash, personal_care,
gifts_donations, loan_emi, other_spend, uncategorised (not yet labelled), card_spend_unexpanded
(a card bill paid from a bank account whose card statement has not been read).
