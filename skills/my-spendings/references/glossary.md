# Glossary

One line each, used at the end of every report and reply.

- **Savings rate**: share of income left after spending, tax and money sent to family.
- **Investing rate**: share of income that went into investments (net of money coming back).
- **FD (Fixed Deposit)**: money locked with a bank for a fixed time at a fixed interest rate.
- **UPI (Unified Payments Interface)**: India's instant phone-to-phone payment system.
- **NEFT / IMPS / RTGS**: bank-to-bank transfers (batch, instant, and large-value).
- **NACH / ECS (auto-debit mandate)**: a standing permission that lets a company pull money from your account on a schedule.
- **Add-on card**: an extra card on your credit-card account for someone else; its spending is billed to you.
- **Fuel surcharge**: a ~1% fee added to fuel bought by card; some cards waive it within limits.
- **Annual fee waiver**: the card's yearly fee is dropped if you spend above a set amount.
- **GST (Goods and Services Tax)**: tax added to bank and card fees (18%).
- **Forex markup**: an extra percentage charged on payments in a foreign currency.
- **Diversification**: spreading money across different kinds of investment so one bad one hurts less.
- **Asset type**: a kind of investment: FDs, stocks, mutual funds, bonds, gold, cash.
